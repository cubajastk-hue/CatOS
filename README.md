# catOS

A cozy, cat-themed web OS in your browser.

Welcome to **catOS**! I built this cute little desktop environment because I wanted a relaxing, brown & creamy-themed space to write down notes, listen to chill beats, play mini-games, and vibe. It runs 100% in your browser using plain HTML, CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript—no heavy frameworks or complex setup required.

 **[Try it out here!](https://cubajastk-hue.github.io/CatOS/)**

##  What can it do?

*   **Interactive Loading Screen:** A custom boot overlay featuring catOS branding, animated spinners, real-time progress percentage, and a soft "Tap anywhere to open" prompt once ready.
*   **catTemp (Theme Templates):** A customization window allowing you to switch between four cozy background themes (*Coffee*, *Cat Car*, *Coffee Beans*, and *Barista Cat*). Automatically     saves your favorite look to `localStorage`.
*   **Tic-Tac-Toe Mini-game:** A playable desktop widget for quick gaming breaks.
*   **Draggable Windows:** Move windows around like a real OS. They stay neatly constrained so you can't lose them off-screen or behind the taskbar.
*   **catNote:** A quick note-taking app that automatically saves everything to your browser's `localStorage`, so your thoughts are right where you left them.
*   **catOfy:** A mini music player loaded with lo-fi tracks and purring sounds, complete with play/pause toggles.
*   **Cozy Aesthetic:** Coffee & cream palette (`#EFDDCC`, `#6C5C48`), custom scrollbars, crisp rendering, and cat icons everywhere.
##  How to run it

### 1. In your browser
Just jump straight into the [Live Demo](https://cubajastk-hue.github.io/CatOS/).

### 2. Locally on your PC
1. Clone the repo:
   ```bash
   git clone [https://github.com/cubajastk-hue/CatOS.git](https://github.com/cubajastk-hue/CatOS.git)
   ```
2. Open `index.html` in your browser. That's literally it!
